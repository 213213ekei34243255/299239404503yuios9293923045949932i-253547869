## What these numbers are (and are not)

| Tier | What runs | What it proves | What it does NOT prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Deterministic scenarios that drive the real controllers (perception, coordinate mapping, CDP mouse/keyboard, verification, safety) in a real Electron `<webview>`. **No model.** | The hands and eyes work: clicks land where intended at DPR/zoom variants, canvases, iframes, drag-and-drop, dialogs, uploads, injection defenses, emergency stop, takeover. | Anything about model quality. |
| **B** | The real `NoahAgent` loop (observe → decide → act → verify → recover, budgets, checkpoints, routing, failover, confirmation flow) driven by a **scripted reference policy** standing in for the model. **It is not an LLM.** | The orchestration, recovery ladder, safety gating and bookkeeping behave as designed. | That any real LLM will pick these actions. **No real-LLM success rate exists in this repository.** |
| **Integration** | The real Jonah app (`main.cjs` + `index.html`), a legacy Rexy goal routed into Noah, the real `OpenAIProvider` over HTTP to a local scripted server. | The wiring in the shipped app: preload, IPC, tab bridge, overlay, legacy event relay, no CDP debugger left attached. | Live cloud endpoints or model behaviour. |
